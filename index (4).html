🚦 Gate

Today: Red gate. Bullish tactical entries are blocked.

Red no longer means “ignore bearish opportunity.” It means normal bullish entries are blocked. Bearish or hedge ideas must go through the separate Bearish Lane checklist.

📉 Bearish Lane

When Red gate is active

Use this section only for bearish or protective hedge ideas. This does not approve trades automatically.

Allowed for review
  • Bear put debit spreads
  • Bear call credit spreads
  • Defined-risk bearish verticals
  • Protective hedge spreads
  • Unleveraged inverse ETF ideas
Still blocked
  • Bullish entries
  • Bull put credit spreads
  • Bull call debit spreads
  • Short stock
  • Naked options
  • Ratio spreads / infinite max loss

Required checks

  1. Today’s Gate row is logged.
  2. Setup is bearish or protective hedge.
  3. Strategy is defined risk.
  4. Max loss is known and fits account risk.
  5. Broker supports the spread cleanly.
  6. Expiration and strikes match the written plan.
  7. Credit/debit meets the minimum requirement.
  8. Stop loss and take-profit plan are written.
  9. Broker ticket matches exactly.
  10. Bernard manually approves before submission.
Decision: If every check passes, candidate may move to Ready Candidate — Bearish Lane. If anything is missing, keep Blocked / Needs review.

🚨 Exceptions

Review rejected orders, missing stops, stale alerts, broker mismatch, duplicate tickers, and unresolved confirmations before new trades.

👀 Watchlist

Ideas come from OptionsPlay 360, Options Alert, Stock Alarm, Pro Ticker, TradingView, OptionsPro, TradeStation, and manual research.

Signals feed Watchlist. They do not bypass gate, scoring, risk fit, broker ticket match, or manual approval.

✅ Ready Trades

Ready means the candidate passed checks and is prepared for Bernard’s final manual review. Ready does not mean submitted.

Bernard manually submits every broker order.

📈 Trades / Reconciliation

Submitted / pending / working is not Open. Only Filled / Executed broker confirmation can become Open after match.

🔔 Alerts / Parsers

Capture TradingView, Pro Ticker, Options Alert, Stock Alarm, broker confirmation, OptionsPlay 360, and OptionsPro examples before trusting automation.

🧪 Testing Mode

Testing mode remains active until go-live blockers are cleared. No live trades during test-only steps.

🏛️ Foundation

Foundation portfolio is separate from tactical trading. Do not mix long-term allocations with trade candidates.
